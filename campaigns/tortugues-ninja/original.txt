Hero’s in a half shell!

The TMNT campaign is run as a campaign with all
rules en characters in this book. It’s possible to play
the campaign with your own league, but then it
won’t be TMNT.

Welcome to New-York city the home of the
Teenage mutant ninja turtles; Leonardo, Raphael,
Donatello and Michelangelo. These four brothers
are fighting a never ending battle against threats
from outer space, the villainous Foot clan and
there leader Shredder. It’s Turtle Time!

Before we get to the campaign this book contains
several new characters and gangs. The new gangs
and Characters are used during the TMNT campaign
but can be used into other Pulp alley games.

Niels

What you need
You are well on your way to experiencing fierce
Turtle action and excitement, you own a copy of
‘Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles” ( TMNT for short).
However TMNT is not a stand-alone product and to
make full use of it , you must own a copy of the Pulp
Alley rules and Pulp leagues .

How to use this book

In addition to the Pulp Alley rulebooks, you will need
a Fortune deck, the TMNT character cards included in
this book, dice, a measuring tape, miniatures to
represent the turtles, there allies and there enemies,
some terrain, a clear gaming area about 3’ x 3’, and
an opponent. Several scenarios in TMNT include
special terrain features or Mutant/alien creatures;
you will need to have markers, counters or
miniatures to represent them on the tabletop.

The Campaign is divided into three Acts, much like
you favorite Teenage mutant ninja turtle episode,
comic or film. In Acts I and III, the scenarios are
played in order. In Act II outcome of some scenario’s
defines the next scenario that needs to be played
next. In Act I once certain objectives have been met,
the campaign proceed to the next act.

Pizza plot point:
The Turtle brother love pizza especially Mikey (
Michelangelo) some games have pizza plot points.

a Pizza slice if they control a pizza plot point. If the

able to use one reroll in any game they wish. The can
collect the pizza slices for more rerolls ( 8 slices gives
2 rerolls etc).

any free sewer marker. A sewer marker is free if no

Alternative settings
This Campaign is written with the Turtles in mind. But
you can make your own gangs and let some other
hero’s fight against an evil villain.
Gotham City - The Batman, Robin, Batgirl and
Nightwing are on the prowl for Joker and his
Henchmen. It has come know to Batman that the
Joker has got his hands on a large amount of ACEChemicals and is trying to use it for some nefarious
scheme. But the great detectives has found clues to
the joker being controlled by a mysterious figure. The
plot can be almost the same. Sewer marker can be
used still. The pizza plot point can be Clues and with
4 clues the bat family will gain 1 re roll.

Harry Potter – Draco Malfoy is trying to smuggle al
sorts of Magical prank material into Hogwarts. It’s up
to Harry and his Friends to make sure his classmates
don’t get pranked by Malfoy. In the meantime they
discovery that you-know-who is trying to get control
of Hogwarts via Draco.

Spy-Fi - The super spy 008 and his loyal friends have
found out that the Russian oil baron is collecting a
strange type of Oil that he will need for a Doomsday
device to hold the world ransom. What 008 and
Money Quid don’t know yet is that “The Russian” is
but a mere henchman of the great “Tsar of Crime”

The Sewer markers can be Switched to secret
entrances or magical staircases. The Pizza markers
can be schoolbooks or O.W.L.S Each time the pass a
“test” the get a letter O or W,L or S. if the got a
complete O.W.L.S test the Gryffindor students will
get a reroll.

The sewer markers can still be used. The pizza plot
points can be clues, just like in the Gotham City
setting.

Si-fi – On a distant planet in a Galaxy on the outer rim
of the know universe a group of rogues join to gather
to ride there home planet of an evil warlord who is
trying to get hold of precious metals only found on
this planet. What the rogues don’t know is that the
warlord is controlled by an Alien overlord who’s
hivemind controls half the universe.
The sewer maker can be portals and the pizza
markers can be smuggle cargo. With 4 pieces of
smuggle cargo they get a reroll.

Getting Started

In act I everything revolves around mutagen.
Footclan needs to collect it and the Turtles need to
stop it.

The Turtles want to get rid of the Footclan once and
for all. The great rise in Footclan activity has given
them motivation to do this.

If the Turtles control a mutagen plot point, this
counts as a normal plot point with normal rewards.
They got it before the Footclan can.

Shredder is in deep trouble and needs to get enough
mutagen to make sure he can continue with his plan
and meet his money lending man the mysterious
Mr.K.

If the Footclan controles a mutagen plot point, they
will get the reward as normal but also roll for
mutagen mnits. The roll is the amount of mutagen
unit the Footclan will add to their total of mutagen
units. See the table below what dice to roll to gather
mutagen units.

The scenarios in act I will normally follow up.
Scenario 1 than 2 etc. if scenario 4 is played continue
with Act II. Act II can be activated sooner if the
Footclan fulfills there objective before scenario 4.
Footclan objective:
If the Footclan at any point gathers more than 35
units of Mutagen/ooze automatically start with act II.
If the turtles collect all 5 Mystery message from Mr.K
they will get a bonus in act III
